1. What Stonk Arena is

Stonk Arena is a free stock market simulation game. All trading uses virtual capital. No real money is deposited, invested or at risk. Nothing in the app constitutes investment advice; prices are shown for game purposes only.

2. Accounts

  • You need an account with a valid email address (or Google sign-in) to participate.
  • One account per person. Creating multiple accounts to gain an advantage is prohibited.
  • Player names are unique and must not be offensive or impersonate others.
  • You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. We recommend enabling two-factor authentication.

3. The monthly competition

  • Every player starts each calendar month with €10,000 in virtual capital.
  • A maximum of 30% of your portfolio may be invested in a single stock.
  • A minimum of 5 trades in the month is required to qualify for the prize.
  • The player with the highest verified return at the end of the month wins the cash prize (currently €100), as announced in the app.
  • Portfolios reset automatically at the start of each month.

4. Prize payout

  • The prize is paid by SEPA bank transfer. The winner must provide a valid IBAN within 14 days of being contacted; otherwise the prize may pass to the runner-up.
  • Payouts are made within 14 days of confirmation.

5. Fair play & verification

  • The organiser's decision regarding winners, exclusions and payouts is final and binding.
